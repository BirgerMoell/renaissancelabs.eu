I — The premise

Not a postcard.
Not a souvenir.

In 1420 a goldsmith with no training as an architect began a dome that the experts of his city had called impossible. He built it without scaffolding. Nobody announced a golden age first. A few people decided to start.

Europe has since learned to treat its renaissance as somewhere to visit: roped off, ticketed, behind glass. We think it is something you do. The first one came from workshops where art, science and craft shared a bench, and from people who assumed they were allowed to try.

The tools have changed. The assumption is the part worth rebuilding.

Rebirth is a verb.
